About

I am a Backend AI Engineer with 3+ years of experience, 2 of which have been dedicated to building production-grade services powered by Generative AI. Currently at Kuehne+Nagel, I design and deploy AI agents that automate internal processes across one of the world's largest logistics companies.

Previously at Agentifai, I fine-tuned and deployed LLMs for low-latency voice agents in healthcare and banking, built multi-agent conversational AI engines, and owned the ML serving infrastructure. Before that, I built hardware abstraction layers at Smartex for AI-powered textile inspection, and NLP pipelines at Skorr.

I hold a Master's degree in Software Engineering from the University of Minho, specializing in Distributed Systems and Knowledge Engineering. I care about building AI systems that are practical, reliable, and deliver real-world impact.
